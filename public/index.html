How it works
Three steps. Any country.
Set up your dietary profile once. Then just point your camera.

Scan
Point at any barcode, food label, restaurant menu, or street food dish. Foral reads it instantly — even in languages you can't.
2
Understand
AI translates every ingredient, cross-references it with your dietary profile, and gives you a clear safety verdict in seconds.

Shop safely
Browse pre-filtered grocery catalogs by country. See only products that match your diet — with direct links to buy.
